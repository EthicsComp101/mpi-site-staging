Fund Your Team

Grants and local funding that schools, teachers and parent groups can use to cover ethics competition entry fees and coaching. Pick your country and area.

How we can help

  1. Find a fund below and check the deadline.
  2. Email us and we'll send a quote, a letter of support and draft wording for your application.
  3. For entry fees and coaching, tell us your goals and we'll work something out with you. Fee support is also available while you apply.
Would you like us to come to your school? We run talks, workshops and help starting an ethics team. See school visits.
Can't find anything for your area? Email us and we'll look with you.
Contact: astapleton@modusponensinstitute.com
South Africa and across Africa: most teams combine school support, local donations and one suitable grant. Write a short budget (entry fees, coaching, data, travel), ask partner schools to approve a contribution through their governing body, and invite alumni or local businesses to sponsor a named team. Many CSI programmes and foundations fund ongoing learner programmes rather than one-off events, so present debate as a programme and check each funder's rules on competition costs. NPO registration helps, and in South Africa SARS PBO and Section 18A approval let donors claim tax deductions. You don't need a grant before entering: ask us about fee support.
Brazil and Latin America: most public funding in Brazil is for public schools. Private schools usually fund teams through local sponsors, Rotary clubs and parent crowdfunding. Ask us about fee support.
