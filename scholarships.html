Scholarships & Fee Waivers

We offer fee waivers and reduced rates for students and schools who need them, across our competitions, classes and coaching. Tell us a little about your situation and we'll work something out.

01 · Competitions

Entry fee waivers

Full or partial waivers for TKEthics Invitationals, Ethics Olympiads and the Global AI Debates, for individual students or whole school teams.

02 · Classes & camps

Reduced tuition

Scholarship places and reduced rates for our live philosophy classes, camps and one-on-one tutoring.

03 · Schools

Coaching for under-resourced schools

Support for schools starting an ethics or debate team, including discounted coaching and free preparation resources.
