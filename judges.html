Judges

Judge an MPI ethics event

Our judges question students from around the world on hard ethical cases, then score their reasoning and give feedback. Events run online, so you can join from anywhere.

Sign up or tell us your availability

How it works

Step 1

Tell us when you can judge

Fill in the form and choose the events that suit you. There's no account or dashboard to set up.

Step 2

We confirm by email

We review each sign-up, confirm which events you're judging, and send the schedule ahead of the day.

Step 3

Train, then judge

New judges join a short training on Zoom before their first event. On the day, we run the timing and send you to your rooms.

Which events suit you

Paid honorarium

TKEthics Invitationals and Ethics Olympiads

For judges with a philosophy background. You'll question teams on the case, score each round and give spoken feedback.

Volunteer

Global AI Debates

Open to a wider range of backgrounds, including technology and policy. Review recorded speeches and essays in your own time, or judge the live finals on Zoom.
