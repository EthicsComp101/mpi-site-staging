What is an ethics competition?

Teams of three to five students discuss a real dilemma in front of judges. Teams can agree with each other. Judges look for clear reasoning, a fair hearing of other views and thoughtful answers to questions.

Getting started

  1. Start a team

    Invite three to five students and choose a coach.

    First practice checklist
    • Set a date for your first meeting.
    • Choose a case and send it to the team.
    • Ask everyone to bring a view and one reason for it.
    Find funding if you need it →

  2. Pick an event

    Check the age range, region and registration deadline before you choose.

  3. Learn the theories

    Try three ways to reason through a decision before you practise a round.

  4. Practise the cases

    Read a case together and take turns responding to another team’s reasoning.
