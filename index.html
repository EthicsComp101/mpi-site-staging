Partners

Who we work with

Official licensee

Ethics Olympiad

MPI is an official licensee of the Ethics Olympiad competitions, running the Pan American, Pan Atlantic and European Ethics Olympiads.

Grant funder

Future of Life Institute

AI safety research and education, co-founded by Dr. Max Tegmark of MIT. Backs Global AI Debates with a $10,000 grant.

Sponsor

Marc Sanders Foundation

Sponsors our Case Writing Prize and supports philosophy education in schools.

Network

Philosium

Global network of young philosophers across six continents and 60+ countries. Partner in making philosophy accessible.

Co-organizer

TKEthics

Worldwide ethics invitationals and host of the International Grand Final.

Debate partner

SuperDebate

Public debate nights, local debate clubs and online debate for everyone. Co-hosts SuperDebate Boston with MPI.

Model UN partner

KSMUN

Korea Students Model United Nations, Seoul (21–22 Nov 2026). We share each other’s events with our students and delegates.
