Philosophy classes

Live online courses in philosophy, ethics, and logic, from four-day camps to ongoing classes. Students read classic and contemporary arguments, learn how to analyze them carefully, and practice building clear arguments of their own in small, discussion-based groups.

Explore classes

Tournaments

Ethics Olympiads, TKEthics Invitationals, and Global AI Debates for students ready to test their reasoning in public. Teams compete online with students around the world, receive judge feedback, and may qualify for international finals.
