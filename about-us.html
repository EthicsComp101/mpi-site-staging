Founders

CO-FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Archie Stapleton

Archie Stapleton holds an MTS from Harvard Divinity School and has spent a decade coaching debate and philosophy internationally. He runs MPI Education, a philosophy and debate academy for students around the world, whose programs include the Ethics Olympiads, the TKEthics Invitationals, and the Global AI Debates.

CO-FOUNDER & HEAD COACH

Zach Bloom

Zach recently completed his Masters in Philosophy from UBC, receiving the Canada Graduate Scholarship-Master's Award for his research in mathematical ontology. He specializes in formal logic and metaphysics, but has extensively studied ethics and political philosophy. For over five years, Zach has professionally coached debate, earning major victories for his students across North America and China.

Coaching team

Our coaches

TKETHICS COACH & ETHICS OLYMPIAD JUDGE

Jack M. Wells

Adjunct professor at The New School for Social Research and PhD candidate (Aristotle Onassis Prize Fellow), with First Class Honours from the University of Sydney. Jack researches race in international law and early 20th-century global diplomacy, and is a longtime Ethics Olympiad judge and facilitator.

TKETHICS COACH & ETHICS OLYMPIAD JUDGE

Mika Smith

PhD student in philosophy and science studies at the University of California San Diego, with a BA in philosophy and psychology. Mika researches reasoning, values, and methodology in science, including AI, and is an Ethics Olympiad judge and former Lincoln-Douglas debater.

TKETHICS COACH & GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Calvin Xu

Calvin studied philosophy at Northwestern University, graduating magna cum laude, and spent a year reading philosophy at Oxford. He is now a JD candidate at the University of Chicago Law School. He has coached debate and ethics for over five years, and led the first Canadian team ever to compete at the International Ethics Olympiad, winning silver.

PUBLIC FORUM DEBATE COACH

Fiona Shen

Fiona studies Economics and Public Affairs at Brown University and has over 4 years of experience coaching debate. Representing Brown, she qualified for the World University Debate Championships. Her expertise lies in the intersection of economic theory and persuasive communication, helping students master complex global topics.

PUBLIC FORUM DEBATE COACH

Nikki Zheng

Nikki Zheng studies political science and economics at Columbia University. Over six years of competitive Public Forum, she ranked in the top 1% of debaters internationally, was the top-ranked PF debater in Canada, and placed at Stanford, Harvard, Princeton and the Tournament of Champions. She holds the NSDA Award of Premier Distinction. Nikki has coached many debaters on her own school team, and she coaches in both English and Mandarin.

PUBLIC FORUM DEBATE COACH

Lillian Xue

Lillian studies Psychology and Data Science at Northwestern University, where she is an active member of the Parliamentary Debate Union. She has over three years of coaching experience, leading students to elimination rounds at international tournaments, and has broken at national tournaments including the Harvard and Georgetown Invitationals.

Judges

Our judges

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Brian Boyce

Brian holds a BA (Hons) in philosophy and mathematics and a Graduate Diploma in Philosophy, with interests in the philosophy of logic and the philosophy of time. He has judged the Ethics Olympiad across Australia, New Zealand, Hong Kong and India for five years, and TKEthics events for two.

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Adam Godwin

Hailing from Oxford in England, Adam Godwin (MA) is a master of metaphysics and an expert in epistemology who has been involved in philosophy and ethics education for over a decade. Specialising in bizarre and unexpected questions and highly tangential philosophical discussions, he is always looking for students who express truly original ideas and a capacity to think in unconventional ways.

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Kyla Coutts

Kyla Coutts holds a BA in Philosophy with a Certificate in Ethics and an MA in Philosophy from Western University, where her research explored the ethics of being known. A former President of the University of Western Ontario Debate Society and a CUSID Impact Award recipient, she coaches debate and judges Ethics Bowl.

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Robert Finan

Robert graduated from Trinity College Dublin with First Class Honours in Philosophy in 2026, having begun his degree in Mathematics and Philosophy. His research focuses on the analytic tradition, spanning the philosophy of mathematics, mind and language, logic, and metaphilosophy. He is running an initiative to introduce Philosophy to Ireland's Leaving Certificate curriculum, and recently won the Thomas Clarke Medal as Global Winner at the Global Undergraduate Awards.

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Jay Thakkar

Jay Thakkar holds an MA in Philosophy with a specialization in Samkhya. Outside of academia, Jay is a photographer and the host of the Jay Thakkar Show, an academic podcast.

ETHICS OLYMPIAD & TKETHICS JUDGE

Pranshul Bohra

Pranshul Bohra is a philosophy student at the University of Edinburgh, focusing on ethics, with a particular interest in communication and congruence (a concept coined by Carl Rogers).

ETHICS OLYMPIAD JUDGE

Brian Palmiter

Asst. Director of Student Engagement, Safra Center for Ethics

Brian Palmiter is an Adjunct Lecturer in Public Policy at the Harvard Kennedy School and Assistant Director of Student Engagement at the Edmond & Lily Safra Center for Ethics. He coaches the Harvard Ethics Bowl Team and advises the Harvard Undergraduate Ethics Society. His research sits at the intersection of ethics, democratic theory, and public policy.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

John Connor

Founder, SuperDebate

John Connor is the founder of SuperDebate, a league of local debate clubs and live championships. A city-wide varsity debate champion from Chicago's South Side, he debated in college on a full scholarship, then spent a decade building products, advising technology companies on AI strategy, and founding ventures including Debate Academy Online.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Jonathan Barry

Director of Policy, Office of Yoshua Bengio

Jonathan Barry is Director of Policy to Professor Yoshua Bengio and the Vice President of Global Government Affairs and Public Policy at LawZero. He has advised two Canadian Prime Ministers and two Ministers of Defence, and holds a Master in Public Affairs from Princeton University.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Ben Levinstein

Alignment Researcher, Anthropic

Ben Levinstein is an alignment researcher at Anthropic. He works on formal epistemology, decision theory, and the philosophy of AI, and has held posts at Rutgers, Bristol, and Oxford's Future of Humanity Institute.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Priten Soundar-Shah

Visiting Researcher, Harvard Department of Philosophy

Priten Soundar-Shah is an educator, philosopher, and entrepreneur working at the intersection of technology and education, and a visiting researcher at the Harvard Department of Philosophy. He leads PedagogyFutures, Academy 4 Social Civics, and Thinker, and is the author of AI & The Future of Education (Wiley, 2023) and Ethical Ed Tech (Wiley, 2026).

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Paula Boddington

Philosopher of AI Ethics

Paula Boddington is a philosopher with a long record of interdisciplinary work in applied philosophy, including the ethics of AI. She has held research and teaching posts at Bristol, the Australian National University, Cardiff, and Oxford. Her books include Towards a Code of Ethics for Artificial Intelligence (Springer, 2017) and AI Ethics: A Textbook (Springer, 2023).

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Joseph A. Getto

International Fellow, Democratic Resilience Center

Joseph A. Getto holds a master's degree in International Conflict Resolution and Coexistence from Brandeis University's Heller School, specializing in arms control, nuclear weapons, AI, and international security. He is an international fellow with the Democratic Resilience Center at Helmut Schmidt University, and previously served as a Specialist Research Fellow with the Alva Myrdal Center for Nuclear Disarmament.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Nelson Spence

Founder, Project Navi

Nelson Spence is the founder of Project Navi, where he builds software and conducts independent research in AI security, formal verification, and dynamical systems. His work examines how intelligent systems behave and what can be rigorously established about them. Drawing on seven years of behavioral health research and peer support, he brings a perspective grounded in autonomy and self-determination to questions of AI development and governance. His central concern is whether technology strengthens people’s capacity to understand, decide, and act for themselves.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Emma Soulanticas

Jr. Community Analyst, Improve the News Foundation

Euthymia (Emma) Soulanticas is a Jr. Community Analyst at the Improve the News Foundation, where she leads VerityEd, providing free media literacy tools. A former member of the Greek National Debate Team and Best Overall Speaker at the Eurasian Schools Debating Championships, she studied Political Science at Leiden University.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Ana Silva

Head of Marketing, Verity

Ana Silva is Head of Marketing at Verity, an AI-powered news platform focused on transparency and media literacy, where she leads the VerityEd Media Impact Lab. A multilingual journalist and University of Toronto graduate, her work centers on algorithmic and media literacy education.

GLOBAL AI DEBATES JUDGE

Jenna Besh

Philosophy & Communication, UCLA

Jenna Besh is a senior at UCLA studying Philosophy and Communication, where she captains the Intercollegiate Ethics Bowl team and is President and Founder of Black Bruins in Philosophy. She is a research assistant with Education for Flourishing at Saint Louis University, focusing on youth wellbeing. Her interests include ethics, philosophy of religion, and the First Amendment, and she plans to attend law school next fall.
