The philosophy and ethics of AI, for teens

Can a machine think? Should an AI ever have rights? Are there jobs AI should never do? This course gives students the philosophical tools to reason clearly about the technology shaping their lives. It's taught by coaches who research AI and values, and who run the Global AI Debates, an international student competition on AI ethics.

What students learn

Minds and machines: the Turing test, the Chinese room, consciousness

AI and moral status: could AIs be persons?

Fairness and bias in algorithms

Work, automation and responsibility

AI safety and governance: who decides?

How to build and defend an argument on AI questions

Format: small group or 1:1, weekly, 60 minutes, live on Zoom. Students can finish by entering the Global AI Debates.
