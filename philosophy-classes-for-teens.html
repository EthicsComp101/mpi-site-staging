Philosophy classes for teens

For students who keep asking "but why?". Each class takes a big question, looks at what great philosophers have said, and gives students the space to argue their own view.

Topics: what can we know; the mind and consciousness; free will; does God exist; personal identity; justice; the good life.

Format: small group or 1:1, weekly, live on Zoom.

Coaches: Archie Stapleton, Jack Wells and Zach Bloom.
