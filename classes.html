Questions

Who are the classes for?

Students aged about 11–18 who enjoy big questions, arguments and discussion. No background in philosophy is needed for our introductory classes. Advanced classes suit students preparing for competitions or university study.

How are classes taught?

Live on Zoom, in small groups or 1:1. Classes combine short readings, discussion, structured argument practice and feedback.

How much do classes cost?

It depends on the format (group or 1:1) and how many sessions you want. Book a free consultation and we'll suggest options. Fee support is available for students from under-resourced schools.

Can my school or academy book a class?

Yes. We teach classes for schools, academies and clubs worldwide, and can prepare teams for ethics bowls, the Ethics Olympiad and the Global AI Debates.

What time zones do you teach in?

Our coaches are based across North America and Europe, so we can usually find a time that works from the Americas, Europe, Africa or Asia.
