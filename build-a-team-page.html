Build your team's fundraising page

One page that parents, relatives and local businesses can read in two minutes: who you are, what the money does, and how to give. It's free, it lives on Google Sites, and it takes about 30 minutes.

1Get your own copy of the template

We've built a ready-made page (see the example below). Google Sites only lets editors copy a site, so we make the copy for you: email info@modusponensinstitute.com with your school, team name, state and the Google account to share it with, and you'll get your own editable copy, usually the same day.

See the example page →   Or start a blank Google Site

Starting from blank? Create the site, add a banner with your team photo, then paste the text blocks from step 3. Using a school Google account? Ask your IT office if Google Sites is turned on; a personal account works too.

2Put your team in the banner

Click the top photo, choose Change image, and upload a picture of your team: faces, a practice session, your last competition. Then change the big title to your team's name.

  • Get the school's OK and parental consent before you use any student photo.
  • Pick a photo that still looks good cropped wide on a phone.

3Replace everything in [square brackets]

The template has five short sections. Click any text to edit it. If you're starting from a blank site, paste these in.

About us

[Team name] is [School]'s ethics team. [One or two sentences: who you are and what you're working toward this year.]

What your support pays for

- Competition entry fees
- Coaching and practice materials
- Travel to in-person events

How to give

[Add your school's donation link or instructions here. For example: checks made out to [School] with "[Team name]" in the memo line.]

Local businesses

Want to sponsor the team? We'll thank you on this page. Get in touch: [email]

Questions

[Coach name], [email]

4Sort out how the money comes in

  • Talk to your school first. Most schools can take donations for a club through the office or an online payment page, and some states give donors a tax break for gifts to public schools. Ask what the school needs on the page (who to make checks out to, what to write in the memo, any deadline) and write it into the "How to give" block.
  • Independent team with no school account? Use an online donation service and be clear on the page about who holds the money and what it pays for.
  • Grants: look up your state on Find grants and mention any you're applying for.
  • Sponsor letter: download the template on Raise money, fill it in, and link it from your page.

5Publish

Click Publish (top right). Choose a short web address, for example sites.google.com/view/lincoln-ethics-team. Set Who can view to Anyone. Click Publish again. Show the finished page to your school before you share it.

6Share it

  • Post the link in the school newsletter and parent group chats.
  • Print a flyer with a QR code for the school office, the bookstore and local shops. Email info@modusponensinstitute.com with your page link and we'll make you a one-page flyer with a QR code, free.
  • Walk into three local businesses with the sponsor letter and the page on your phone.
  • Update the page after each event: results, photos, thank-yous to sponsors.

Need a hand? Email info@modusponensinstitute.com with your school and state and we'll help you set the page up. Nothing on this page is tax advice.
