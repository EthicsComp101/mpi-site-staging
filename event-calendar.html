17Nov2026 Boston Debate Showcase: MIT faculty debate AI at Laugh BostonOpen to the public. Laugh Boston, 425 Summer St, Boston (Westin Boston Seaport, next to the convention center). In person

MIT faculty, one stage. SuperDebate and Laugh Boston present an evening of live debate and conversation about artificial intelligence, with audience questions and a reception in the Seaport.

Time
Doors and reception 5:30 PM · program 6:00 PM · ends 9:30 PM ET
Eligibility
Open to the public. Laugh Boston, 425 Summer St, Boston (Westin Boston Seaport, next to the convention center).

Featured exchange: US–China AI competition. Where should the US draw the line between scientific cooperation with China and restrictions meant to protect national security? Yasheng Huang (Epoch Foundation Professor of International Management, MIT Sloan) takes on the hardest trade-off in AI policy.

Also on the program: Manolis Kellis (Professor of Computer Science, MIT; MIT Computational Biology Group at CSAIL and the Broad Institute), who uses AI to understand and treat human disease.

One seat on stage is still open: Manolis Kellis's opponent is yet to be announced. Know who should take him on? Nominate them: astapleton@modusponensinstitute.com

Every session opens to the room, so bring your questions, and stay for the reception to keep arguing it out.

Presented by SuperDebate × Laugh Boston, hosted by John Connor, with Boston host Archie Stapleton and the Modus Ponens Institute.

21Nov2026 UWC Ethics Invitational 2026Grade 7 and up · Worldwide · Online Competition

A student-run online ethics tournament organised by the Model Philosophy Ethics Olympiad leadership team at UWCSEA Dover, in partnership with TKEthics. A regional qualifier: the top three teams advance directly to the TKEthics International Grand Finals.

Time
Sat 21 Nov, 6:00 AM - 12:15 PM ET / 7:00 PM SGT - 1:15 AM SGT (Sun 22 Nov) / 10:00 PM AEDT - 4:15 AM AEDT. Online on Zoom.
Eligibility
High school teams from anywhere: 3-5 students, Grade 7 or above. No debate experience needed.

Format: four live rounds on Zoom, two cases per round. Each case runs as an ethical presentation, collaborative commentary, a response and judges' questions. Teams are scored on careful, honest reasoning, not on winning an argument.

Cases: UWC Ethics Invitational 2026 case package (PDF)
