Our History

Zach Bloom and Archie Stapleton met on their first day of university in 2016. A decade of philosophy degrees, debate coaching and running competitions later, MPI Education teaches philosophy, ethics and logic to students around the world, and runs three international ethics competitions, in partnership with the technology and ethics non-profit TKEthics.

800+schools competed across the Ethics Olympiads in 2025
60+Ethics Olympiad teams in MPI's divisions
40+TKEthics Invitational teams from four countries
75+Global AI Debates entries from around 100 students

Timeline

  1. 2014

    The Ethics Olympiad comes to Australia

    Archie's high school philosophy teacher brings the US National High School Ethics Bowl to Australia as the Ethics Olympiad. Archie is on the first ever Australian Olympiad team.

  2. 2016

    Zach and Archie meet

    First day of university at UBC. Both go on to honours degrees in philosophy, then Zach an MA in Philosophy at UBC and Archie an MTS at Harvard Divinity School, while coaching debate throughout.

  3. Jul 2023

    The John Kleinig Scholarship Fund

    A gift from philosopher John Kleinig creates a scholarship fund for students and schools who couldn’t otherwise afford philosophy training: competition entries, camp places and International Philosophy Olympiad preparation.

  4. Sep 2024

    The Modus Ponens Institute is founded

    Zach and Archie incorporate MPI with one simple aim: to improve public discourse and education through philosophy, ethics and dialogue.

  5. 2025

    Ethics Olympiads for the Americas and Europe

    Under a licence from founder Matthew Wills, MPI begins running the qualifying Olympiads for the Americas and Europe. Teams from MPI's divisions go on to win or place second at the International Final.

  6. 2025

    The TKEthics Invitational and Global AI Debates launch

    Two new competitions open to students anywhere: the TKEthics Invitational, an Ethics Bowl-style event for teams in any formation, and the Global AI Debates, for speeches and essays on AI and society.

  7. 2026

    Grants from the Marc Sanders Foundation and the Future of Life Institute

    A Marc Sanders Foundation grant to TKEthics funds fee waivers, free coaching and outreach for under-resourced schools. A Future of Life Institute grant funds prizes and fee waivers for the Global AI Debates. Our first Senior European Ethics Olympiad takes place.

  8. Summer 2026

    Camps, Shanghai and a Novice Invitational

    Live philosophy camps online, AI ethics and debate camps in Shanghai, and our first Novice Invitational for newer competitors.

  9. Fall 2026

    The Marc Sanders Foundation Case Prize

    Students write the ethics cases that future competitors will debate, with US$1,500 in prizes each round: two winners, each receiving $250, with $500 to each winner’s school philosophy programme.

Ethics Olympiads

School teams of 3 to 5 study prepared cases, then discuss them with another team under questioning from qualified judges. Top teams qualify for the International Final.
