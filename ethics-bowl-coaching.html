Ethics Bowl and Ethics Olympiad team coaching

We run and judge the TKEthics Invitationals and the Pan-American, European and Middle School Ethics Olympiads, so we know exactly what judges look for. Coaching covers ethical theory, reading a case set, the presentation and commentary structure, and answering judges' questions.

Format: team sessions, weekly in the run-up to an event. Fee support is available for under-resourced schools.
