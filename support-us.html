Why it matters

Every year we hear from teachers who have a room full of curious students and no money to enter them. A state school in the UK. Public schools in South Africa and the United States. A bilingual public elementary school in Hungary. Students in Ghana, Egypt, India and the Philippines.

When they get the chance, these students do remarkable work. They read difficult cases, argue both sides, and stand up in front of judges from universities and AI labs to defend what they think is right. Many of them have never done anything like it before.

Donations and grants close that gap. Since 2023 they have paid for fee waivers, free coaching, camp places and prizes, so that who takes part depends on curiosity, not on cost.

Our mission is simple: to improve public discourse and education through philosophy, ethics and dialogue, for every student who wants to take part.MPI Education, in partnership with TKEthics

Where your gift goes

02

Training sessions for schools starting their first ethics or debate team, taught live by graduate-level coaches.

03

Prizes & publication

Prize money and published work that give students real recognition, and a reason to keep going.

04

New communities

Reaching schools in countries and neighbourhoods where competitive philosophy has never been offered.

Who we work with

Our students come from public schools, international schools, debate academies and home-school groups across North America, Europe, Africa and Asia. Our judges include researchers and philosophers from universities and AI organisations, and our work has been supported by partners who share the belief that every student deserves a chance to think carefully about the future.
