School Visits

We come to your school. Our team teaches philosophy and ethics in classrooms, helps schools start ethics teams, and helps them host their own ethics competitions. We travel to schools, or we can run the same sessions on Zoom.

Get in touch about a visit →

What we can do

Philosophy and ethics talks. An engaging session for a class, a year group or an assembly, built around a real ethical question.

Hands-on workshops. Students work through ethics cases in small groups, build arguments and test them against each other.

Starting an ethics team. We help teachers set up an Ethics Bowl or Ethics Olympiad team and run its first practice sessions.

Hosting an ethics competition. Want to host an ethics bowl at your school? We help with the format, cases, judges and running the day.

Teacher training. Practical training for teachers who want to bring ethics discussion and case work into their own classes.

Who we are

MPI runs international ethics competitions for secondary students, including the TKEthics Invitationals and the Ethics Olympiad events, and coaches school teams all year. Our visiting team is made up of philosophy graduates and PhD researchers who teach and judge these events.

In October 2026 we ran an in-person ethics event in Chandler, Arizona: a morning training workshop followed by three rounds of real ethical cases, judged by philosophers.
