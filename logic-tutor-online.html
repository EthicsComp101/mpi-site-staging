Logic tutoring with a specialist

Logic is the toolkit for every other subject: it shows you exactly why an argument works or fails. Zach Bloom, MPI's logic specialist, completed his Masters in Philosophy at UBC with a Canada Graduate Scholarship for research in mathematical ontology, and has spent five years coaching students to championship results.

What students learn

Arguments, validity and soundness

Propositional logic: truth tables, natural deduction

Predicate logic and quantifiers

Informal fallacies and how to spot them in real arguments

Applying logic to debate, essays, maths and computer science

Who it's for: students preparing for university philosophy, maths or CS; debaters; anyone taking a logic course at school or university who wants support.

Format: 1:1 or small group, weekly, 60 minutes, live on Zoom, with exercises between sessions.
