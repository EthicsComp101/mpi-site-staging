2026 Senior European Ethics Olympiad

On March 19th, MPI held its first-ever Senior European Ethics Olympiad. We are thrilled with how the day went and incredibly proud of every team that took part. This year's competition was especially significant: we welcomed teams from Morocco for the first time, bringing a genuinely international dimension to the European region. Congratulations to all seven teams who competed with such seriousness and skill.
