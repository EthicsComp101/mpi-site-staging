Now Open: Marc Sanders Foundation Case Writing Prize

Awards outstanding student-written ethics cases on technology and ethics. Winning submissions may be included in future TKEthics Invitational case sets.

Prize

$1,000 USD to the student's school for ethics and philosophy education

$500 USD to the student author or authors

MPI may divide prizes across up to three winning entries

How to submit

Cases should be 300–600 words with three discussion questions beneath the case
