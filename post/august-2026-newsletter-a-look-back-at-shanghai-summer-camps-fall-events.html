A look back at Shanghai

We spent July in Shanghai teaching ethics, AI and competitive debate. With support from Zhonggu Charity and the Marc Sanders Foundation, we ran an AI and Ethics camp where students learned core ideas in ethical theory and then put them to work designing and coding their own AI-powered apps.

For the final two weeks, Zach Bloom and Archie Stapleton coached Public Forum debate and guided students through the National High School Debate League Championships. Our students earned a number of breaks across the middle school and novice divisions, improved enormously over the two weeks, and we had a blast doing it.

New: Intro to Ethics Competitions

A four-day intensive for students who want to compete with confidence. We will cover the major ethical theories, build sharper arguments, learn the competition format, and work through all six cases from the TKEthics Novice Invitational before finishing with full practice rounds and feedback.

You can use the skills for Ethics Bowls, Ethics Olympiads, TKEthics Invitationals or the Ethics Cup. Come as an individual and we will place you on a team.

See the full camp and book

Prepping for an Olympiad?

If you or your team are preparing for an Ethics Olympiad, Ethics Bowl or TKEthics Invitational this fall, we are available to help with coaching sessions, case walkthroughs and full practice rounds. Just get in touch and we will set something up.
