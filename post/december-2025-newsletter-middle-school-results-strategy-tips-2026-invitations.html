Welcome to the December Newsletter!

As we move into the Senior season, here is a tip for teams looking to improve their scores:

While it is not necessary to be an expert in ethical theories, and we don't want teams to name-drop philosophers, we do think that understanding the logical requirements of different frameworks is vital. A common issue judges see is a team stating they are using a Deontological framework (based on moral duties and rules), but then arguing entirely using Consequentialist logic (focusing solely on the positive or negative impacts of an action), or vice versa.

To make a really consistent argument, your justification must match your framework.

We would be pleased to offer your school training, even in person at your school (we have coaches all over the world), but please feel free to access our free class package here: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1nS4wFvGmo-B0adYJfRLkstplzWHfmPI3
