November 2025 Newsletter: TKEthics Fall Invitational Highlights

Archie Stapleton

Nov 17, 2025

1 min read

Welcome to the November edition of our Newsletter! We have many updates we're excited about, as well as the results from our latest event on November 8th.

There are limited places available for the TKEthics Invitational Spring 2026, and registrations have begun. Register here, and find the case set here. Finally, this is the full invitation.

We're very pleased to announce a new 10% discount for any institutions which register 3 or more teams for any given event across MPI or TKE.

TKEthics Invitational: Top 8 Teams

Gold: Diogenes (Wellington College) – 55.75

Silver: Hume – 54.75

Bronze (tie): Nussbaum & Wollstonecraft (GE Elite) – 53.33

5th (tie): Nagarjuna (Sands Secondary, Canada) & Augustine (Bethpage High School) – 52.00

7th: De Beauvoir – 51.17

8th: Laozi (Central Unified School District, CA) – 50.50

Honorable Mentions: Diogenes, Hume, Wollstonecraft, Nagarjuna, Augustine, Laozi, Spinoza (UWC), Confucius (Basis Flagstaff)

Junior Spark Award: Nussbaum, Wollstonecraft, Bulter (WeUp): top middle-school teams recognized for courage competing against more senior opponents.

The Top 8 teams qualify for the 2026 International Grand Final in August.

Philosophy joke of the month:

A dean asks the head of the physics department to see him.

"Why are you using so many resources? All those labs and experiments and whatnot, this is getting expensive! Why can't you be more like mathematicians - they only need pens, paper and a trash bin. Or philosophers! They only need pens and paper!"
