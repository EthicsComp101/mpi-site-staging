Ethics for high school students

What makes an action right or wrong? Students learn the major ethical theories, then use them on real cases, from medical dilemmas to online privacy, in the same discussion style used at ethics bowls and the Ethics Olympiad.

What students learn

Theories of the good and of right action

Utilitarianism and consequentialism

Kantian ethics and rights

Virtue ethics

Applied cases, and how to weigh competing views fairly

Format: small group or 1:1, weekly, 60–90 minutes, live on Zoom.
